Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Heuritech, a Luxurynsight company – Paris, France

Oct. 2023 - Now

CTO of Heuritech, a Luxurynsight AI technology providing trend data to the fashion industry.

  • Modernization strategy: audited a stack that had barely changed in 6 years while data volumes doubled every year and could no longer scale. Defined and led a multi-year modernization roadmap focused on the main bottlenecks and risks, around the data architecture and the overall infrastructure.
  • New data architecture:
    • Introduced Snowflake as the central data warehouse, replacing a 50-node Elasticsearch 2.4 cluster, not suited to our needs and that could no longer scale. Secured the main critical pipeline that turns AI predictions into trend data.
    • Moved all data flows from unmaintained frameworks to scalable processes in Airflow and Celery.
  • AI inference at scale: built a new inference stack on NVIDIA Triton. It can process 10 billion images in a few weeks, where the legacy stack would have needed 2 years of continuous compute.
  • Cloud migration: moved from 100+ unmaintained on-premise Kubernetes nodes to AWS (EKS & MongoDB Atlas), redesigning workloads for the cloud. Delivered under budget, without service interruption.
  • Maintained product delivery: shipped the full roadmap alongside the migration.
  • Team: restructured the tech organization around key senior hires (DevOps, data engineering, data science, backend), with almost no turnover.
  • AI adoption: drove the adoption of AI-assisted engineering.
